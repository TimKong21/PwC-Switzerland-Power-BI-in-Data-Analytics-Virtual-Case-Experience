KPI definitions

What each retention measure means

Churn rate
Customers marked Yes in the supplied Churn field divided by customers in the selected group.
Tenure
Customer tenure field in months. The site presents group averages, not a prediction score.
Technical tickets
Mean of the supplied technical-ticket count. Ticket volume requires operational context before it is used for targeting.
