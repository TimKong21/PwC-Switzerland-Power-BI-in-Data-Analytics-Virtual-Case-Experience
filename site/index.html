Cross-functional leadership decisions

One operating rhythm, three decisions

  1. Protect customer access. Operations leaders review unanswered demand and call-topic performance before assigning improvement work.
  2. Prioritise retention outreach. Commercial and care leaders use observed segment differences to define a testable intervention cohort.
  3. Strengthen the leadership pipeline. HR and executive sponsors review representation, promotion outcomes and the decision rules behind talent actions.
Decision principle: the figures identify questions for review; they do not establish causation or prescribe an outcome. Each detailed page makes the source, definition and proposed next step explicit.

Methodology & assumptions

Evidence-led, static and transparent

All displayed figures are calculated from the supplied case workbooks or shown as existing portfolio-case evidence. No live Power BI report, public embed, backend service or unverified KPI is used.

Source coverage

Call-centre records cover 5,000 calls; customer records cover 7,043 customers; the workforce case dataset covers 500 employees.

Analytical limits

These are historical portfolio-case datasets. The visual comparisons surface patterns for investigation and should not be read as live operational measures or causal proof.

Delivery standard

Recommendations are framed as analyst playbooks: signal, question, action, accountable owner and success measure—ready for a stakeholder discussion.

Data and original case assets: original GitHub repository.
