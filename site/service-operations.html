KPI definitions

What each measure means

Answer rate
Answered calls divided by total calls, calculated for the case period and each topic.
Resolution among answered
Calls marked resolved divided by calls marked answered. It avoids treating an unanswered call as an attempted resolution.
Average satisfaction
Arithmetic mean of the recorded satisfaction-rating field; the source workbook does not supply a target benchmark.
